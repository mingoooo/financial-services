# Trend Template

Use this checklist to decide whether the stock belongs in a Minervini-style buy zone at all.

## Core Checklist

Score each item as pass, borderline, or fail:
1. Price above 150-day moving average
2. Price above 200-day moving average
3. 150-day moving average above 200-day moving average
4. 200-day moving average rising or flattening after a long decline
5. Price above 50-day moving average
6. Price reasonably close to 52-week highs
7. Relative strength strong versus the broad market and preferably versus peers
8. Prior uptrend exists before the current base

## Interpretation

- **Pass**: Most items pass and no major structural fail exists.
- **Borderline**: One or two items are weak, but the structure is repairing; treat as watchlist, not immediate action.
- **Fail**: Multiple structural items fail, especially long-term moving-average alignment or lack of prior uptrend.

## Practical Notes

- A stock can pass the template and still be too extended.
- A stock can have excellent fundamentals and still fail because institutions are not yet in control.
- Treat template failure as a capital-preservation signal, not an invitation to debate the story.
