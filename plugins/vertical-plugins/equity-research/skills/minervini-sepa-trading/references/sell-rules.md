# Sell Rules

## Defensive Exit Framework

Prioritize defense first.

Core rules:
- Predetermine the stop before entry.
- Exit failed breakouts quickly instead of waiting for the story to repair itself.
- Respect abnormal reversals or heavy-volume breaks of support.
- Reduce aggression when the general market weakens and leading stocks start breaking.

Questions to ask:
- Is the original setup still valid?
- Has the character of the stock changed from tight/constructive to loose/distributive?
- Is the market making failure more likely even if the stock-specific story is intact?

## Offensive Exit Framework

Protect gains without mechanically cutting every winner too early.

Use offensive selling when:
- The stock becomes climactic after a rapid advance.
- Price stretches far beyond logical support.
- The name starts stalling repeatedly after a large move.
- A partial sale helps keep you aligned with the broader plan.

## Common Mistakes

Avoid:
- Averaging down after a failed breakout
- Moving stops lower to avoid taking a loss
- Turning a position trade into a long-term hold without evidence
- Confusing hope with process
